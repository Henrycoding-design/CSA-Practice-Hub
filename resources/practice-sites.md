# Practice Sites

## CodingBat

- [CodingBat Java](https://codingbat.com/java) is best for short repetition with strings, arrays, loops, and recursion.
- Recommended sections: `Warmup-1`, `String-1`, `Array-1`, `Logic-1`, `Recursion-1`.

## Runestone

- [CSAwesome 2](https://runestone.academy/runestone/books/published/csawesome2/index.html) follows the newer AP CSA curriculum framework.
- [CSAwesome v1](https://runestone.academy/ns/books/published/csawesome/index.html) remains useful for 2019-2025 style review.

## IDEs

- [BlueJ](https://www.bluej.org/) works well for first exposure to objects and classes.
- [IntelliJ IDEA](https://www.jetbrains.com/idea/) is stronger for larger Java projects.
- [Replit Java](https://replit.com/languages/java10) is useful for browser-based quick practice.

## Practice Routine

1. Do five small problems.
2. Write down one repeated mistake.
3. Redo the hardest problem without looking.
4. Finish with one FRQ-style trace.
