# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving constructive feedback with care.
- Focusing on what is best for learners.
- Showing empathy toward students, teachers, and contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Public or private intimidation.
- Sharing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for harmful behavior.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a GitHub Issue. If the report involves sensitive personal information, share only the minimum necessary detail and request maintainer follow-up.
